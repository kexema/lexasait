# Google Ads, UX, and trust audit

## Source analyzed

The supplied gist is an MHTML snapshot of `https://betakastro.pro/` with a Slovak retirement and estate-planning theme. It includes a traditional multi-page service-site structure with home, about, services, gallery, process, portfolio, testimonials, and contact links.

## Key risks found

- **Thin and generic content:** multiple cards use generic headings such as “Feature,” which looks auto-generated and weakens perceived legitimacy.
- **Mixed-language UX:** Slovak copy is mixed with English labels such as “Gallery,” “Our Process,” and “Founder & CEO,” creating quality and trust issues.
- **Suspicious template fingerprints:** randomized class names, MHTML snapshot artifacts, and generic stock-style sections make the site feel cloned or autogenerated.
- **Unclear business model:** the original page presents professional financial/estate support, but it does not clearly explain operational boundaries, disclaimers, or who provides regulated advice.
- **Google Ads trust gaps:** legal pages, privacy details, cookie notice, direct company information, and transparent disclaimers are either absent or not prominent enough for a regulated-adjacent category.
- **Weak proof quality:** testimonials are generic and not tied to a credible product workflow. Metrics are broad satisfaction claims rather than product reliability or security indicators.
- **SEO and semantic issues:** legacy static markup lacks a modern app metadata system, structured schema, clear heading hierarchy, accessible components, and a coherent content strategy.

## Redesign strategy

The rebuild preserves the core idea—helping people organize estate, legacy, and family planning materials—but reframes it as a premium AI SaaS workspace. This lowers policy risk by positioning the product as software for organization and collaboration rather than a provider of guaranteed legal, tax, financial, or retirement outcomes.

## Implementation principles

- Premium SaaS visual language: minimal layout, glass cards, gradients, large typography, product mockup, and whitespace.
- Trust-first copy: no “guaranteed,” “quick money,” fake urgency, countdowns, or exaggerated claims.
- Compliance-style transparency: Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, About, Contact, cookie notice, data deletion language, no-professional-advice disclaimers.
- Conversion structure: hero, social proof, 8 features, onboarding flow, security/trust section, metrics, testimonials, FAQ, and CTA.
- Technical foundation: Next.js App Router, React components, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion reveal animations, metadata, Open Graph tags, JSON-LD, responsive layout, and accessible focus states.
